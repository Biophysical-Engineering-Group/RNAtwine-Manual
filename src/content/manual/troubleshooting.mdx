## The relax does not start because loops have no role

<Figure name="faq-no-role" />

The relax starts only when every kissing loop has a role, and a loop that the drawing leaves open has none unless the assembly joins it ([Loop roles](../concepts/#loop-roles)).
Set each loop under **Needs a decision** in Build's **Kissing loops** table to **close**, **open** or **join**, or set them all with **Set all**, and click **Save roles with design** so that later runs keep the roles.
On the command line, give `--role KEY=ROLE`, or save the roles with `rnatwine loops <design> --role KEY=ROLE --save`.

## Edit says "Not relaxed: loops with no role."

A loop that had no role when editing began holds back the relax after each edit, and the role you give it in the panel is saved with the design ([Edit](../edit/#loop-roles-during-edits)).

## I have runs that are not saved

<Figure name="faq-unsaved-badge" />

**Save all unsaved...** in Export writes every unsaved run into a folder at once, and <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>) saves the run on screen.
Unsaved runs are lost when you open another design or when they drop out of the session's list ([Runs and run files](../export/#runs-and-run-files)), so save before you switch designs.

## The relax ended before it converged

The metrics in [Inspect](../inspect/#metrics) say so in red, and the termination is a label other than `converged`.
The structure is the answer of the relax, but its energy is not that of a minimum, so do not compare it with the energies of converged runs.

Because a relax usually fails to converge when the design's energy landscape is rugged, with many shallow minima close together, the remedy to try is to adjust the design in [Edit](../edit/) and relax it again.

## A loop is not formed although it looks closed

Because "KL formed" tests twist register and not distance, a loop closed in distance can still be out of register, which the table in [Inspect](../inspect/#kissing-loops) calls mis-registered and shows with its gap and twist error.

## Thermal motion or Full atom is grayed

The tooltip of a grayed button gives the reason, so hover over it first.
[Thermal motion](../inspect/#thermal-motion) lists what the motion needs, and **Full atom** needs a relaxed run, so it is grayed until the first relax.

## The colors are gray

Gray marks what the color mode in use cannot color: with flexibility, anything but the latest relaxed structure, and with **vs reference**, the nucleotides that the comparison did not match ([Colors](../inspect/#colors)).

## pyFuRNAce was not found

Your own pyFuRNAce is needed for two things: to open a pyFuRNAce `.json` that has no up-to-date export beside it, and to write the `.json` of an edited design.
The message lists each Python the app tried ([A pyFuRNAce .json](../open/#a-pyfurnace-json)), and the fix is to give the path of the Python that has pyFuRNAce under **Where pyFuRNAce is** in Edit.
Everything else works without pyFuRNAce: open the design's `.dat` instead of its `.json`.

## A design does not open

The card of the design in Open gives the reason, and since a design needs at least its `.dat` and its `.txt`, side by side under one name, a group of files that lacks one of them is listed as incomplete.

## The energies of two runs cannot be compared

Because the parameters change what the energy measures, energies can be compared only between runs of the same design under the same parameters.
The run comparison in Inspect marks such energies as not comparable, and such runs are compared instead by their convergence, clashes, loops formed and fit to a structure.

## A run file opens with "scored ... under this version (saved ...)"

The run was saved by another version of RNAtwine, whose model gives a different energy at the same coordinates, so relax the design again to get a result of this version.

## Relax into map asks for the map's resolution

The blur of the map and the weight of the map term depend on the resolution, and the field is filled in only when an EMDB header beside the map (`emd-N.xml`) or a header label states it.
Type the resolution from the map's EMDB entry, or put the entry's header file beside the map, and on the command line give `--map-resolution`.

## Models of one map came out in opposite hands

The fit by shape chooses a hand for each model, and at low resolution the arrangement of a large design can fit the wrong hand better.
Since a map has one hand, set **Hand** (`--map-hand`) to the hand that the map's deposited model sits in, so that every model compared in that map takes the same one.

## The fitted PDB does not lie on the map in ChimeraX

When the hand is the mirror image, the PDB is in the frame of the map's mirror image, so run `volume flip #1 axis x` on the map and look at the PDB in the flipped map ([Export](../export/#structures-of-a-run-relaxed-into-a-map)).
The line above Export's buttons and the `MAP FRAME` records say which frame a file is in.
A PDB that had to be moved to fit PDB's columns no longer lies on the map, as the status line says when it is written, so write mmCIF instead.

## The relax into a map turned helix steps left-handed

The `handedness` line of the block counts the stacked steps that the map pulled through to left-handed, which means that the map's weight is too strong for it.
Keep **Noise** at residual and lower the **Weight scale** ([Judging a fit](../inspect/#judging-a-fit)).

## A map with a mask or a flattened solvent

A masked or post-processed map has little or no noise left in its solvent, and a weight read from the solvent's noise would then be far too strong.
The default noise, **residual**, is read from what the placed model leaves of the map within its reach, so the solvent's spread does not set it, and the exact zeros of a mask are left out of the solvent, as the `noise` line counts.
So keep **residual** for such a map, and use **solvent** only for a map whose solvent keeps its noise.

## Save is grayed for a run relaxed into a map

A run file does not store the relax into a map, so such a run is kept by writing its fitted structures in Export ([Keeping the result](../inspect/#keeping-the-result)).

## The design opens labeled AS IMPORTED

The view shows the coordinates of the imported structure until a relax puts its kissing loops and stems on the model ([Importing an experimental structure](../open/#importing-an-experimental-structure)).

## The design is no longer in view

**Reset view** in the Camera window frames the whole design again.

## The relax takes much longer than expected

When a relax runs past its usual range, the strip under Relax says so ([Relax](../build/#relax)).
Designs of one size differ that much in how long they take, and assemblies run several relaxes to search their starts and states.
More CPU cores in **Settings** let those searches run more relaxes at once.
