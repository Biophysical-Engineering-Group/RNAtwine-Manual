## The relax does not start because loops have no role

<Figure name="faq-no-role" />

A relax starts only when every kissing loop has a role, and a loop that the drawing leaves open has none unless the run's assembly joins it ([Loop roles](../concepts/#loop-roles)).
Set each loop under **Needs a decision** in Build's **Kissing loops** table to **close**, **open** or **join**, or set them all at once with **Set all**.
So that later runs keep the roles, click **Save roles with design**.
On the command line, give each loop a role with `--role KEY=ROLE`, or save the roles with the design by running `rnatwine loops <design> --role KEY=ROLE --save`.

## Edit says "Not relaxed: loops with no role."

A loop that had no role when editing began holds back the relax that follows each edit.
Give that loop a role in the panel, and the role is saved with the design ([Edit](../edit/#loop-roles-during-edits)).

## I have runs that are not saved

<Figure name="faq-unsaved-badge" />

**Save all unsaved...** in Export writes every unsaved run into a folder at once, and <kbd>Cmd</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>) saves the run on screen.
Because unsaved runs are lost when you open another design or when they drop out of the session's list ([Runs and run files](../export/#runs-and-run-files)), save them before you switch designs.

## The relax ended before it converged

The metrics in [Inspect](../inspect/#metrics) show this in red, and the termination label is something other than `converged`.
The relax still returns its structure, but that structure is not at a minimum, so do not compare its energy with the energies of converged runs.

Because a relax usually fails to converge when the design's energy landscape is rugged, with many shallow minima close together, try adjusting the design in [Edit](../edit/) and relaxing it again.

## A loop is not formed although it looks closed

Because "KL formed" tests twist register and not distance, a loop whose halves are close together can still be out of register.
The table in [Inspect](../inspect/#kissing-loops) calls such a loop mis-registered and shows its gap and twist error.

## Thermal motion or Full atom is grayed

The tooltip of a grayed button gives the reason, so hover over the button first.
[Thermal motion](../inspect/#thermal-motion) lists what the motion needs.
**Full atom** needs a relaxed run, so it is grayed until the first relax.

## The colors are gray

Gray marks what the color mode in use cannot color: with flexibility, anything but the latest relaxed structure, and with **vs reference**, the nucleotides that the comparison did not match ([Colors](../inspect/#colors)).

## pyFuRNAce was not found

RNAtwine needs your own installation of pyFuRNAce for two things: to open a pyFuRNAce `.json` that has no up-to-date export beside it, and to write the `.json` of an edited design.
The message lists each Python that the app tried ([A pyFuRNAce .json](../open/#a-pyfurnace-json)).
To fix it, give the path of the Python that has pyFuRNAce under **Where pyFuRNAce is** in Edit.
Everything else works without pyFuRNAce, so a design can be opened from its `.dat` instead of its `.json`.

## A design does not open

The card of the design in Open gives the reason.
Because a design needs at least its `.dat` and its `.txt`, side by side under one name, a group of files that lacks either of them is listed as incomplete.

## The energies of two runs cannot be compared

Because the parameters change what the energy measures, energies can be compared only between runs of the same design under the same parameters.
The run comparison in Inspect marks the energies of any other pair of runs as not comparable, and it compares those runs instead by their convergence, clashes, loops formed and fit to a structure.

## A run file opens with "scored ... under this version (saved ...)"

The run was saved by another version of RNAtwine, whose model gives a different energy at the same coordinates.
To get a result of this version, relax the design again.

## Relax into map asks for the map's resolution

The blur of the map and the weight of the map term depend on the resolution.
The field is filled in only when an EMDB header beside the map (`emd-N.xml`) or a header label states the resolution.
Type the resolution from the map's EMDB entry or put the entry's header file beside the map, and on the command line give it with `--map-resolution`.

## Models of one map came out in opposite hands

The fit by shape chooses a hand for each model, and at low resolution the arrangement of a large design can fit the wrong hand better.
Since a map has one hand, set **Hand** (`--map-hand`) to the hand that the map's deposited model sits in, so that every model compared in that map takes the same hand.

## The fitted PDB does not lie on the map in ChimeraX

When the fit chose the mirror hand, the PDB is in the frame of the map's mirror image, so run `volume flip #1 axis x` on the map and look at the PDB in the flipped map ([Export](../export/#structures-of-a-run-relaxed-into-a-map)).
The line above Export's buttons and the `MAP FRAME` records say which frame a file is in.
The status line says when a PDB had to be moved to fit the fixed columns of the PDB format, and such a PDB no longer lies on the map, so write mmCIF instead.

## The relax into a map turned helix steps left-handed

The `handedness` line of the block counts the stacked steps that the map pulled through to left-handed.
Any such step means that the map's weight is too strong for the design.
Keep **Noise** at **residual** and lower the **Weight scale** ([Judging a fit](../inspect/#judging-a-fit)).

## A map with a mask or a flattened solvent

A masked or post-processed map has little or no noise left in its solvent, so a weight read from the solvent's noise would be far too strong.
The default noise, **residual**, is read from what the placed model leaves of the map within the model's reach, so the spread of the solvent does not set it.
The exact zeros of a mask are also left out of the solvent, and the `noise` line counts them.
For a masked or flattened map, keep **residual**, and use **solvent** only for a map whose solvent keeps its noise.

## Save is grayed for a run relaxed into a map

Because a run file does not store the relax into a map, keep such a run by writing its fitted structures in Export ([Keeping the result](../inspect/#keeping-the-result)).

## The design opens labeled AS IMPORTED

The view shows the coordinates of the imported structure until a relax puts the structure's kissing loops and stems on the model ([Importing an experimental structure](../open/#importing-an-experimental-structure)).

## The design is no longer in view

**Reset view** in the Camera window frames the whole design again.

## The relax takes much longer than expected

When a relax takes longer than its usual range of times, the strip under Relax says so ([Relax](../build/#relax)).
Relax times differ this much between designs of the same size, and assemblies run several relaxes to search their starts and states.
Allowing more CPU cores in **Settings** lets those searches run more relaxes at once.
