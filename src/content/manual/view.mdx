## The toolbar

<Figure name="view-toolbar" />

On the left, the toolbar over the 3D view chooses what is drawn: **Drawn** or **Relaxed** ([Inspect](../inspect/#drawn-or-relaxed)) and the representation, **Coarse**, **Twine** or **Full atom**.
On the right, the toolbar chooses how the structure is drawn, with the **Color** menu ([Colors](../inspect/#colors)), **Thermal motion** ([Thermal motion](../inspect/#thermal-motion)), **View options** and **Camera**.
Every control is always shown, and a control that cannot act is grayed, with a tooltip that says why.

The label under the view names the picture: `AS DRAWN`, `AS IMPORTED`, `RELAXING (stage): iteration n`, `RELAXED, run #n`, `QUICK RELAX, run #n`, `EDITED (n edits), not yet relaxed` or `BLOCKS PLACED (n blocks, m copies), not yet relaxed`.
For a run under parameters other than the standard ones, the label also names those parameters.

## Moving the view

Drag to turn the structure, right-drag or shift-drag to move it, scroll or pinch to zoom, and click a nucleotide to select it ([Selecting a nucleotide](../inspect/#selecting-a-nucleotide)).
So that the design cannot be lost from view, zoom stays between 0.05 and 20 times the distance that frames the whole design, and **Reset view** in the Camera window frames the whole design again.

## Coarse, twine and full atom

<Figure name="view-coarse" />

**Coarse** draws each nucleotide as a sphere, and draws the base pairs, kissing loops, crossovers and clashes unless View options hides them.

<Figure name="view-twine" />

**Twine** draws the same coordinates with each strand as one smooth tube through its nucleotides, shaded as three-ply laid twine, and with the base pairs and kissing-loop bonds as thin rods.
Because the twine takes several times as long as the coarse view to draw, it is a style for still pictures, although clicking a nucleotide selects it as in the coarse view.

<Figure name="view-full-atom" />

**Full atom** draws the all-atom structure of the run on screen in ball-and-stick, relaxed or drawn as the switch says, and so it needs a relaxed run.
The all-atom structure is built the first time you choose Full atom and is kept for the run.
**Cancel build** stops a build, and a stopped build keeps nothing.
Each structure, relaxed or drawn, takes about 2 kB of memory per nucleotide, so a 100,000-nucleotide tube holds about 0.4 GB for the two geometries.

## View options

<Figure name="view-options" />

**View options** opens a window under the toolbar with the same sections in every representation.

- At the top, the caption of the color mode in use says what the colors mean and gives the ends of the scale.
- **Model**: a size and a style, whose meanings depend on the representation.
  In the coarse view they set the radius of the spheres (nm), and in the twine view they set the thickness of the twine relative to the default and its style, laid **rope** or smooth **tube**.
  In the full-atom view they set the size of the balls and their coloring, by element or by nucleotide.
- **Show** sets which of these are drawn besides the nucleotides:
  - **loop labels**, the kissing-loop keys and handles over the view
  - **base pairs**
  - **loop bonds**, the kissing-loop pairs, colored by outcome
  - **bKL bonds**, the pairs of undeclared branched kissing loops
  - **crossovers** and **clashes**
  - **drawn model**, the design as drawn, as a pale overlay
  - **dim the rest**, which fades all but a selection
- **Thermal motion**: the knob that sets the swing ([Thermal motion](../inspect/#thermal-motion))

## Camera

<Figure name="view-camera" />

**Camera** opens a window with these controls in the same place as View options, and the two windows replace each other:

- **Reset view**, which frames the whole design again by moving the camera and nothing else
- **Look**: **Front** (the structure's front, upright), **Long axis** (down the structure's long axis), **Second axis** and **Quarter roll** (the view rolled a quarter turn)
- **Turntable**, which turns the view slowly about the vertical, at a speed from 5 to 90 degrees a second (23 to start)
- **Lighting** ([below](#lighting))

### Lighting

Because the lighting controls show no scales, hover over a control to see its value.
To move the light, drag the brass lamp on the ivory dial.
The lamp's angle around the dial sets the direction the light comes from, and 90 degrees lights from above.
The lamp's distance in from the rim sets how high the light stands, from grazing at the rim to head-on in the middle.
The scroll wheel turns the lamp around the dial, the arrow keys move the lamp once the dial is clicked, and a double click returns the light to its default direction.

Three small knobs, each from 0 to 1, shape the light on the background now on screen, while the other theme keeps its own values:

- **fill**: how light the side turned away from the light is
- **rim**: the light added at the outline
- **outline**: how far the outline is darkened

A double click returns a knob to its default, and the push-button returns the direction and the knobs of both themes to their defaults.
The lighting lasts for the session and is not saved, and an exported image is drawn with it.

## Themes

<Figure name="view-themes" />

Because the model's colors are chosen against a dark background, the app always opens in the dark theme, even when the system is set to light.
The switch at the foot of the rail changes the theme.
So that the model stands out on the light background, it is drawn differently there: the outline is darkened like a pen line instead of lit, and the shadowed side is lighter.
Pale colors, such as the grays, the straw and the gold of the selection, are deepened in proportion to their lightness, while saturated colors keep their full strength.
